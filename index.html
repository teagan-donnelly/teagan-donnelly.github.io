01 / THE PROBLEM

When disasters strike,
information becomes fragmented.

During hurricane recovery, people in need, volunteers offering help, and coordinators often struggle to communicate quickly and effectively. Critical information about urgent needs, available resources, and ongoing operations can become scattered across multiple channels.

Fragmented Communication

People in need, volunteers, and coordinators often use separate channels.

Time-Sensitive Needs

Urgent requests require fast, organized coordination.

Critical Information

Recovery efforts depend on accurate, up-to-date information.

02 / SYSTEM DESIGN

Three roles.
One coordinated response.

The system connects aid requesters, volunteers, and aid coordinators through a Java backend with specialized managers for requests, accounts, volunteers, and shelters.

03 / REQUEST LIFECYCLE

From requesting help
to a completed response.

Requests carry identifiers, assistance types, urgency, locations, and status. The model distinguishes personal assistance requests from infrastructure reports.

  1. Submitted

    Request created with details and location.

  2. Reviewed

    Coordinator reviews the request and assigns urgency.

  3. Assigned

    An eligible volunteer is assigned.

  4. In Progress

    Assistance moves through response stages.

  5. Completed

    Completion is recorded under shelter-manager permissions.

Simplified representative workflow, not an exhaustive state-transition diagram. The enum also includes pending review, approved, awaiting assignment, en route, arrived, denied, and canceled. Cancellation is distinct from completion.

Camellia

Clinical intelligence for
more proactive patient care.

Camellia is an AI-powered healthcare prototype designed to connect fragmented patient records, surface potentially overlooked clinical concerns, and support proactive care coordination. Developed during PalmettoHacks 2026, the platform combines unified clinical search, evidence-linked AI analysis, obstetric anesthesia coordination, personalized audio briefings, and simulated wearable monitoring.

01 / THE PROBLEM

Critical information
gets missed.

Patients with complex medical histories often receive care across multiple providers and record systems. When important information is fragmented, clinical teams may not have the full picture before a scheduled procedure or delivery. Camellia explores how unified information and earlier coordination could support more informed care.

Fragmented records

Important patient information is scattered across systems and can be difficult to find quickly.

Missed risk factors

Relevant clinical history may not be recognized early enough for advance planning.

Delayed coordination

Care teams may lack the shared context needed to prepare for complex cases.

02 / THE PLATFORM

A clearer picture of patient care.

Camellia is a clinical intelligence prototype that brings together searchable patient records, evidence-linked clinical flags, proactive anesthesia coordination, and personalized audio briefings in one interface.

  • Unified Clinical Search

    Search across fragmented patient records.

  • Evidence-Linked Clinical Flags

    Surface potentially overlooked findings with supporting source references.

  • OB Anesthesia Coordination

    Help care teams identify complex upcoming deliveries and prepare earlier.

  • AI Audio Briefings

    Generate concise, personalized patient and shift summaries.

Prototype demonstration uses synthetic patient information. Not intended for clinical use.

01 / THE PROBLEM

Shared financial responsibilities aren’t simple.

Financial decisions rarely affect just one person. Roommates, partners, and families often share expenses and financial goals, but traditional budgeting tools focus primarily on individual finances. Vesta helps people coordinate commitments without exposing their entire financial lives.

Different Relationships, Different Expectations

Roommates, partners, and family members may need different levels of financial visibility.

Privacy Matters

Users can communicate financial readiness without automatically revealing sensitive personal information.

Risks Are Often Unseen

Upcoming shortfalls can be difficult to anticipate before they affect shared commitments.

02 / KEY FEATURES

Plan, protect,
and grow together.

Vesta combines predictive financial forecasting, shared workspaces, and AI-powered guidance to help users make informed decisions and coordinate shared financial responsibilities.

  • Shared Financial Workspaces
  • Predictive Financial Modeling
  • AI-Powered Financial Guidance
  • Relationship-Specific Privacy Controls
  • Interactive What-If Simulations

03 / PREDICTIVE MODELING

See the impact
before it happens.

Vesta’s Domino simulator models hypothetical financial decisions so users can explore how potential changes affect their future balances and shared commitments.
