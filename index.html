Camellia
Healthcare Management System

Palmetto Hacks 2026 · Award-Winning Hackathon Project

Camellia is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to connect fragmented patient records, identify potentially overlooked clinical concerns, and improve proactive patient care. Developed during PalmettoHacks 2026, the prototype integrates clinical search, evidence-linked AI analysis, personalized audio briefings, and simulated wearable monitoring.

View GitHub Repo Business Case Document
Hackathon Project Finished

Project Overview

Camellia began with conversations with healthcare professionals about fragmented patient records, overlooked clinical information, and delayed communication between care teams. Our team developed a functional prototype to explore how AI and connected devices could support more proactive, informed patient care.

What I've Done:

  • Unified Clinical Search: Centralized access to synthetic patient records and clinical histories, designed for future interoperability with existing EHR systems.
  • Evidence-Linked Clinical Flags: Gemini-assisted analysis highlights potentially overlooked patterns without making diagnoses. Findings link directly to supporting documentation for clinician verification.
  • Proactive Care Coordination: Role-based dashboards and specialized obstetric anesthesia alerts help teams identify patients who may require advance planning.
  • Smart Patient Monitoring: A simulated wearable system automatically records encounter-specific observations and generates nursing alerts for mobility, repositioning, and potential falls.

Projects > Vesta

Vesta
Financial Management System

Technica 2026 · Financial Technology Hackathon

Vesta is an AI-powered financial planning platform that helps users anticipate financial risks, simulate spending decisions, and coordinate shared financial responsibilities without sacrificing privacy. Built during Technica 2026, the prototype combines predictive cash-flow forecasting, personalized AI guidance, and relationship-specific financial sharing.

View GitHub Repo Video Demo
Hackathon Project Finished

Project Overview

Vesta was developed at Technica 2026 to address a common financial challenge: managing shared expenses without requiring complete financial transparency. Traditional budgeting tools focus on individual spending, but financial decisions often affect roommates, partners, and other people with shared commitments. Our team developed a functional prototype that combines deterministic financial forecasting with Gemini-powered guidance, helping users identify potential shortfalls, explore solutions, and selectively share financial information with others.

What I've Done:

  • Collaborative Financial Planning: Developed shared financial workspaces for roommates and couples, supporting group expenses, individual commitments, and contribution-readiness tracking.
  • Predictive Financial Modeling: Implemented cash-flow forecasting and what-if simulations to identify potential shortfalls and evaluate financial adjustments before payment deadlines.
  • AI-Powered Financial Guidance: Integrated Google Gemini to provide contextual financial explanations and personalized recommendations, with Backboard supporting opt-in conversational memory.
  • Privacy-Focused Design: Designed customizable, recipient-specific sharing controls so users can selectively disclose balances, upcoming bills, and financial projections.
  • Full-Stack Development: Helped build and refine the application's Next.js and TypeScript interface, financial dashboards, API integrations, and interactive demo experience.

Technical Architecture

Vesta combines a Next.js and TypeScript application with a deterministic financial forecasting engine. Google Gemini provides conversational explanations and financial recommendations, while Backboard supports optional preference memory. The Capital One Nessie API provides access to sandbox banking data. The hackathon demonstration uses consistent fictional financial profiles stored locally in the browser, allowing users to explore shared planning scenarios without affecting real financial accounts.

Highlights: Requirements Engineering, Stakeholder Analysis, UML, Functional & Non-Functional Requirements
